Guide: Essential Digital Safety Rules 01:45 / 03:00

Three Essential Rules to Remember

1. Receiving money NEVER requires entering your PIN or approving an AutoPay mandate.
2. Official student portals only need basic name/email sign-in; never grant Drive or file permissions.
3. Check the end of file names. Scammers hide programs (.lnk, .exe) behind Word or Excel icons.
